1. Core Vision

Cupid's mission:

Help people discover meaningful connections.
Reduce the awkwardness and pressure of traditional dating apps.
Build communities around universities and shared interests.
Allow friendships, networking, and dating to coexist.

Instead of the usual swipe-left, swipe-right conveyor belt, Cupid should feel like entering a digital campus where people are actually living their lives.
